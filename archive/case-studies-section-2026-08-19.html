Excellence Trade · Phoenix Investment House

Next Trade · the first advised-trading launch
The challenge

A brokerage competing in a market where the obvious lever is price, and where personal investment advice had always belonged to the banks. Its clients traded independently, with no professional guidance attached to the screen they were already looking at: every decision about new money, a position to trim, or a portfolio drifting off its risk level fell to them alone.

What they launched

Next Trade, live August 2026, built on Finnovest and delivered inside the Phoenix Investment House app the client already uses. Excellence's investment experts issue a recommendation; the platform resolves it against each client's own holdings and stated risk level, then pushes it to their phone with the trade instruction already prepared. The client approves or declines, and only on approval does the order route to the exchange. Offered at no additional cost to clients with portfolios from ₪20,000, after a six-question profile and a digital agreement. More than a year in development.

Why it matters

The first time independent traders received personal, executable recommendations inside the app they already traded in. Not a model portfolio and not a general market insight, but a personal recommendation created by a professional, authorised investment advisor. The platform resolves each recommendation against the client's own position and lets retail investors stay in control: approve or decline. It also allows new investors to build a portfolio based on the advisor's recommendations, shaped by their own profile.

02

Discount Bank

The challenge

A large retail book and an advisory desk that could only reach clients one conversation at a time. The digital channel carried plenty of information, but nothing a client could act on.

What they launched

Smart Invest: proactive, fully tailored recommendations on single stocks, bonds, ETFs and mutual funds, delivered into the bank's own mobile app. Clients read the reasoning behind each one and authorise the whole set of orders with a single press.

Why it matters

After a quiet release the service went national with a television campaign, and uptake brought many billions of shekels in assets under advisory onto the platform. Advisory stopped being a scheduling problem and became a service the bank could offer everyone.

Recognised with the Digital Transformation Project of the Year award from People & Computers.

03

FIBI · First International Bank

The challenge

A bank whose position rests on investment advisory and a declared customer-centric approach, looking to widen that service without hiring advisors at the same rate.

What they launched

Advise.me, unique to the bank: personal investment recommendations a client receives and can act on directly from the phone, with suitability verification and documentation running automatically behind every one.

Why it matters

Advisory became available around the clock, with no branch visit and no queue, while supervision moved from reviewing trades afterwards to checking them before the client ever sees an action button.

Covered by TheMarker in its feature on innovation at the First International Bank.
