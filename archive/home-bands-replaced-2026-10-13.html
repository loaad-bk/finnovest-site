The advice gap is the growth opportunity.

Today, without Finnovest

Your clients consume advice. Just not from you.

A fast-growing generation of retail investors trades more often, across more assets, and wants guidance at the exact moment of decision. Yet at that moment, they are often on their own. So they turn to social media, online forums and AI tools: unlicensed, unaccountable and blind to their portfolio, risk and goals. Every one of those trades is a decision your advisors could have shaped. The demand for advice has never been bigger. Whoever meets it may be taking the client relationship from you.

What it may cost you

Your institution may hold the account. Someone else may own the client relationship.

Today financial influencers send your client to trade with you. Tomorrow, with someone else who offers a cheaper trading deal. When the advice layer moves outside your institution, influence over client decisions and asset flows might move with it, leaving your institution at risk of becoming execution infrastructure.

Deployment models

Built around what you already have.

Every deployment starts with Finnovest Core, which powers scalable personalized advice and automated trading. From there, institutions can choose how much of the client experience to adopt.

01 · Finnovest Connect

Private deployment

Connect Finnovest Core to your existing systems and use its advice, compliance and trading capabilities behind the scenes. Your institution designs and controls the entire client experience.

Best for banks running their own systems and channels.

02 · Finnovest Embedded

White-label mobile SDK

Add Advised-trading directly into the mobile app your clients already use. Finnovest Core powers the advice and trading logic, while the white-label SDK delivers the full client experience under your brand.

Best for brokerages whose clients already trade in their app.

03 · Finnovest Complete

White-label app

A full-service trading app under your brand that combines independent trading and Advised-trading in one app, with Finnovest Core built in end to end. Clients trade on their own, receive professional advice and execute their recommendations with one tap to Trade-All™.

Best for institutions without an app of their own, or without in-house advisors.
