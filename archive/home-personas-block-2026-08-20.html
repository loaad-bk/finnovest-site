What we mean by hyper-personal

One source, thousands of tailored recommendations.

The same methodology, applied simultaneously to three people who want completely different things from the same market.

Marta, 32

Graphic designer

Not a trader. Runs her own design studio, doesn't follow capital markets closely, but knows her savings should be working properly.

Wants limited risk and the feeling of being on top of it.

John, 45

Real-estate broker

Trades occasionally on his own. Married with two children and short on the time it takes to actively manage a portfolio.

Wants responsible decisions without the research hours.

Steven, 21

College student

Loves trading and will take calculated risks for higher returns. Reads the rationale before he acts, every time.

Wants conviction plus a short reason to go for it.

Marta, John and Steven receive the same underlying advice on the same morning. None of them receives the same instruction.
