About us

Bridging the gap between investment professionals and retail investors.

Finnovest sits at the front of a transformative shift in retail investing, built around the largest wealth transfer in modern history. Our technology lets investment professionals scale personalised advice across the mass-affluent market and deliver a next-generation, non-discretionary advisory experience.

The thesis

Next-gen retail investors want to act independently, and still want to be advised.

Until now, scaling non-discretionary advisory wasn't feasible. Finnovest is the first to deliver the depth of traditional one-to-one advice at one-to-many scale: actionable, tailored recommendations created simultaneously for each client's own portfolio, holdings and risk appetite, arriving in real time and authorised with a single tap.

Preserve the depth of personalised advice while achieving the scale today's market demands.
